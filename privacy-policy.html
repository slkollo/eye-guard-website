Privacy Policy

Eye Care - Your Privacy is Our Priority

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Myungjin Solution Co., Ltd. (주식회사 명진솔루션) ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the Eye Care application on Android and iOS. Eye Care helps parents encourage safer screen-viewing habits using camera-based distance alerts. Features and permissions differ by operating system and app version.

Our contact details appear in Section 11. Please review this Policy together with our Terms of Service.

Important distinction: The camera-based distance check is designed to run on the device. However, the app also offers optional website browsing and in-app purchases, which involve external providers and internet connections. It would be inaccurate to say that no information ever leaves the device.

2. Camera, Face Detection and Distance Alerts

  • On-device analysis: The front camera provides frames to an on-device face-detection component (including Google ML Kit) to estimate whether the device is being held too close. This is face detection, not identifying a person by name.
  • No intentional facial-image uploads: Eye Care does not intentionally send camera frames, facial photos or face templates to our servers for distance monitoring. The app is not designed to save camera video from this process.
  • Warning actions: The app may show an on-screen alert, play a warning sound, count a warning event and record the time of that event for parental analytics.
  • Accuracy: Estimated distance can vary with camera angle, lighting, device and motion. Eye Care is not a medical device.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

According to the features in the current app, Eye Care uses device storage (including SharedPreferences and locally saved files) for the following categories:

  • Parent and child settings: A child display name or nickname, chosen language, distance preferences, warning settings, parental PIN and local recovery answer. We recommend using a nickname and a non-sensitive recovery answer.
  • Optional custom content: A parent-recorded warning voice or selected warning image, if you enable those features.
  • Protection and analytics: Warning counts, timestamps, approximate distance-related values, session durations and charts. Some records may be retained only for the history period supported by your app version.
  • Kids Zone: Parent-added website URLs and, in supported versions, website/domain usage durations and warning counts for websites visited within the Kids Zone browser.
  • Android-specific usage information: If a parent grants Android Usage Access, compatible versions may read app usage information to display monitored-app statistics. This is not the same as access to those apps' private messages or content.

These in-app settings and analytics are intended to be kept locally rather than uploaded to a company analytics account. Operating-system backup features may separately back up certain app data according to device settings.

4. Android and iOS Features and Permissions

4.1 Android

  • Camera: Distance detection while protection is active.
  • Microphone: Optional parent-recorded warning messages.
  • Display over other apps: Where granted and supported, displays eye-distance warnings over other apps.
  • Foreground service: Where permitted by Android and device settings, supports camera-based protection outside the app's visible screen.
  • Usage Access: If requested and granted, supports monitored-app usage summaries.
  • Device Administrator: If offered and voluntarily enabled, supports certain Android anti-bypass or uninstall-related restrictions. It does not authorize us to remotely erase the device.

4.2 iOS (iPhone)

  • Camera: Distance detection while Eye Care's supported screen, including Kids Zone, is active.
  • Microphone: Optional parent-recorded warning messages.
  • Photos: Access to an image selected by the parent for a custom warning screen, when that feature is used.
  • Kids Zone browser: Opens selected websites inside the app and can record local website usage and warning counts for parental analytics.
  • iOS limitations: Eye Care cannot display Android-style warning overlays across unrelated iPhone apps, promise continuous background-camera protection, or prevent the user from uninstalling the app through iOS system controls.

Permissions are controlled in your device settings. Certain features will not work if the related permission is denied.

5. Websites and External Content in Kids Zone

Kids Zone can display third-party websites such as video and social platforms. Those websites are independent of Eye Care. When opened, they may receive standard internet information (such as IP address, browser details, cookies, logins or activity) and may display their own advertisements, login prompts or age restrictions under their own policies.

Eye Care may locally summarize the site/domain, time spent and number of Eye Care warnings while the website is open. These summaries do not mean we can see private messages, passwords or activity inside unrelated installed apps. Parents should supervise which websites children may access.

Website icons (Google favicon service): When Kids Zone loads a site icon, Eye Care may request a favicon from Google's favicon service (for example, google.com/s2/favicons). The website domain is included in that request; Google may also receive standard connection data such as your IP address and request metadata. This is separate from locally stored Kids Zone usage summaries.

6. Purchases, Subscriptions and External Service Providers

Optional purchases are processed by Google Play on Android or the Apple App Store on iOS. Eye Care also uses RevenueCat to check purchase status and manage access to paid features.

  • Possible purchase-related data: App-user or installation identifiers, store transaction or purchase identifiers, product IDs, purchase dates and subscription/entitlement status may be processed by the relevant store and RevenueCat to deliver, restore and verify purchases.
  • Payment cards: We do not directly process or store your full payment-card number; the relevant app store handles checkout.
  • Service diagnostics: App stores and SDK providers may process technical or diagnostic data under their own terms and privacy practices. The precise data involved depends on the installed SDK versions and device settings.
  • Support contact: If you email us or send a deletion request, we receive the email address, message and any order details you voluntarily include.

See RevenueCat Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy and Parental Supervision

Eye Care is designed for use under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. The parent should perform initial setup, manage the PIN and approve any Kids Zone websites. We do not intentionally operate an advertising network directed at children or offer an in-app social profile. Third-party sites accessed through Kids Zone have their own data practices and age requirements.

If a parent believes that information about a child has been sent to us in error, they can contact mjosc@mjosc.co.kr. Compliance with child-privacy laws depends on actual app, SDK and website behavior; this Policy should not be understood as a certification by a regulator.

8. Data Retention, Deletion and Your Choices

  • Local data: You may change some settings in the app or uninstall Eye Care to remove its on-device app data. Removal is subject to operating-system backups, retained store transactions and other system-managed copies.
  • Purchase records: Apple, Google and RevenueCat may need to retain transaction and entitlement records for legal, fraud-prevention or accounting purposes. They have their own deletion and retention procedures.
  • Website data: To manage website cookies, login sessions or data held by a third-party site, use that website's controls and any available app/browser storage controls.
  • Questions and requests: Email us with enough details to locate the relevant support or purchase record. We will assess requests according to applicable law and the limits of data we control.

9. Data Deletion Request

You can ask about deleting data associated with a purchase or support correspondence. This form opens your email app; it does not silently transmit information to a website server. Your request is sent to us only if you choose to send that email. Please do not include passwords, your child's real name or other unnecessary sensitive information.

10. Security and Policy Changes

We use on-device processing to reduce the need for external transmission of camera images. Nevertheless, no application, device backup or network service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and parental PIN protected. We may revise this Policy when functionality, third-party services or legal obligations change; the date above reflects the current published version.

회사 연락처는 제11항에서 확인하실 수 있습니다. 서비스 이용약관도 함께 확인해 주시기 바랍니다.

중요: 카메라를 이용한 거리 확인은 기기 내 처리를 기본으로 설계되었습니다. 다만 키즈존 웹사이트 접속과 인앱 결제는 외부 서비스 및 인터넷 연결을 사용하므로, 모든 정보가 언제나 기기를 벗어나지 않는다고 설명하는 것은 정확하지 않습니다.

2. 카메라·얼굴 감지 및 거리 경고

  • 기기 내 분석: 전면 카메라 영상 프레임을 Google ML Kit 등을 이용한 기기 내 얼굴 감지 기능으로 처리하여 화면이 눈에 지나치게 가까운지 추정합니다. 사람의 신원을 식별하는 얼굴 인식이 아닌 얼굴 감지 기능입니다.
  • 얼굴 이미지의 의도적 업로드 없음: 회사는 거리 감지를 위해 카메라 영상·얼굴 사진·얼굴 특징 정보를 회사 서버로 의도적으로 전송하지 않습니다. 이 과정의 카메라 동영상을 저장하도록 설계되어 있지 않습니다.
  • 경고 처리: 거리가 가까우면 화면 경고와 음성 안내를 제공하고, 보호자 분석용으로 경고 횟수와 발생 시각 등을 로컬에 기록할 수 있습니다.
  • 정확도: 조명, 카메라 각도, 기기 및 움직임에 따라 거리 추정 결과가 달라질 수 있습니다. Eye Care는 의료기기가 아닙니다.

3. 기기에 저장하는 정보

현행 앱의 기능에 따라 Eye Care는 SharedPreferences 및 로컬 파일 등을 이용해 다음 정보를 기기에 저장합니다.

  • 보호자 및 자녀 설정: 자녀 표시 이름(별명), 선택 언어, 거리·경고 설정, 보호자 PIN 및 로컬 비밀번호 복구 답변. 실제 이름보다는 별명, 민감하지 않은 답변 사용을 권장합니다.
  • 선택한 사용자 콘텐츠: 보호자가 직접 녹음한 음성 경고 또는 선택한 경고 이미지.
  • 보호 및 분석 기록: 경고 횟수, 발생 시각, 거리 관련 추정치, 사용 시간 및 차트. 앱 버전별로 지원하는 기간만큼 기록을 보존할 수 있습니다.
  • 키즈존: 보호자가 추가한 웹사이트 주소와 지원 버전에서 키즈존 브라우저로 방문한 웹사이트·도메인별 이용 시간 및 경고 횟수.
  • Android 앱 사용 정보: 보호자가 Android ‘사용 정보 접근’ 권한을 허용한 경우, 일부 버전에서 모니터링 대상 앱의 이용 통계를 조회할 수 있습니다. 다른 앱의 비공개 메시지나 내용에 접근한다는 뜻은 아닙니다.

이러한 설정과 앱 내부 분석 기록은 회사 분석 계정에 업로드하지 않고 로컬에 보관하는 것을 기본으로 합니다. 다만 운영체제 백업 설정에 따라 일부 앱 데이터가 별도로 백업될 수 있습니다.
