Privacy Policy

Eye Guard - Your Privacy is Our Priority

Last Updated: January 15, 2024
IMPORTANT: Eye Guard is committed to protecting the privacy of children. We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any facial recognition data, photos, videos, or personal information. All processing happens entirely on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Eye Guard, a mobile application developed by MJ Solution Co., Ltd. (주식회사 명진솔루션). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Eye Guard application ("App", "Service", "we", "us", or "our").

Eye Guard is designed to protect children's vision by monitoring screen distance using on-device AI technology. We are committed to transparency and protecting your privacy, especially when it comes to children's data.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

We want to be crystal clear about what we DO NOT do:

  • No Facial Recognition Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit any facial images, biometric data, or facial recognition information.
  • No Photos or Videos: The app does not take, save, or upload any photos or videos of your child.
  • No Camera Access Beyond Detection: The camera is used solely for on-device distance measurement in real-time. No images are stored.
  • No Personal Identification: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personally identifiable information from children.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not track or collect location data.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not share any data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. How Eye Guard Works (Technical Explanation)

Eye Guard uses Google ML Kit for on-device face detection. Here's exactly what happens:

  1. Real-Time Processing: When the app is active, it uses the front camera to detect faces in real-time.
  2. Distance Calculation: Our proprietary algorithm calculates the distance between the detected face and the screen.
  3. Instant Alert: If the distance is too close, the app displays a warning overlay and voice alert.
  4. No Storage: All processing happens in your device's memory (RAM) and nothing is saved to storage.
  5. No Internet Transmission: No data is sent over the internet. The app can function completely offline.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Eye Guard is designed for use by children under parental supervision. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar international regulations:

  • No Personal Information from Children: We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.
  • Parental Control: The app requires parental setup and can only be configured/uninstalled by parents using a PIN.
  • No Marketing to Children: We do not display advertisements or marketing content to children.
  • Educational Purpose: The app's sole purpose is to protect children's eye health.
